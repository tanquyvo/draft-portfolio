Vo Tan Quy

I’ve always been curious about the “why” behind the things I see — from a roadside ad to a giant OOH billboard towering over the city. Who is it talking to? What makes people stop and look? That habit of questioning everyday things led me to E-Commerce and pulled me into Marketing, especially where strategic planning and creative thinking meet.

As an E-Commerce student navigating an ever-changing industry, I enjoy connecting the dots between people, insights and ideas. I love exploring the thinking behind a brief, shaping a direction and then getting hands-on to make it happen — bringing people together, organizing the moving parts and turning ideas into real experiences. For me, that’s the fun part: imagining what could be, then figuring out how to make it real.

Strawberry.

a plan with a lot of moving parts!

BRANDKoko Krunch · Nestlé
ROLETeam Leader
LAUNCHJanuary 2027 · Planned
BUDGETVND 3 billion

A go-to-market proposal for the planned launch of Koko Krunch Strawberry in Vietnam. The work connects category and consumer research with a commercial roadmap across five functions — designed to drive trial and brand penetration while keeping the plan grounded in a VND 3-billion budget.

THE CHALLENGE

One launch.
Five functions.
One shared direction.

Turn a new flavour opportunity in the Ready-To-Eat Cereal market into a coordinated launch plan across Marketing, Sales, HR, Supply Chain and Finance — with rapid trial, brand penetration and ROI in mind.

MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTION

From research
to a connected plan.

As Team Leader, I guided the case work and led the overall marketing direction. My contribution included market and consumer trend research, segmentation and target-audience definition, insight development, Big Idea and Key Message development, and collaboration with external teams to refine the strategy.

WHAT I LEARNED

A good GTM plan is more than a collection of department slides. It is a shared operating picture: each function understands its role, timing and contribution to the same business objective.

THE IDEA

Five moods.
One little universe.

Build a product world around Focus, Energy, Calm, Date and Fun — giving each mood its own visual identity while keeping the whole collection recognisably SCENTIE.

MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTION

From product logic
